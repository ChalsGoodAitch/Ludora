1. Install the three components

The parent theme controls presentation. Ludora Booking owns rooms, extras, packages, prices, availability, reservations and payment integration. The optional child theme demonstrates safe customization. The booking plugin can operate with another theme.

  1. Back up the database and wp-content directory. Use a local or staging installation first.
  2. In Appearance → Themes → Add New → Upload Theme, select the installable ludora.zip. Activate it.
  3. Install and activate the optional companion through the theme’s voluntary plugin recommendation, or upload ludora-booking.zip in Plugins → Add New. Never upload the full documentation/source archive as a theme.
  4. Optionally install ludora-child.zip and activate the child after installing its parent. Keep personal PHP/CSS changes in the child.
  5. For payments, install WooCommerce separately from WordPress.org and complete its store setup. Manual booking requests do not require WooCommerce.

The declared minimum is WordPress7.1.4 and64-bit PHP8.4. The tested database is MariaDB11.4.8 with InnoDB; allow at least256MB WordPress/PHP memory and configure HTTPS before real use. Other database/PHP/WordPress versions are unverified. Buyer hosting does not need Node, npm, Composer or Docker.

2. Set up the business

Open Ludora settings. Select an IANA timezone such as Europe/Madrid, contact information, business email, booking terms page and the booking mode. Room opening times use that timezone. Stored intervals use UTC. Review hold duration, manual pending duration and retention days before taking bookings.

Choose English or Spanish in WordPress language settings. Original PHP/UI strings are English; Spanish catalogs and demo content are supplied. The demo importer offers English or Spanish separately. Importing content does not translate your existing business copy.

In Ludora’s demo screen, choose a language and import deliberately. Three example rooms, extras, packages and editable pages use fictional prices and contact details. Repeating an import skips its existing items. Homepage/blog replacement requires its checkbox. Theme activation alone imports nothing. Replace demonstration text, photos, policies, rates and equipment claims before real use.

3. Configure the catalog

Publish a room and enable “Available for booking.” The guided configuration fields save the booking rules; the normal editor saves the description and photos. A published room with booking disabled is not reservable. In the WordPress7.1 editor, open the Meta Boxes drawer at the bottom to reach the guided booking configuration; focus its button and press Enter if needed. Save the post after editing.

Opening endpoints must follow the editor’s same-day validation. Full opening day refers to the configured business opening span, not an unrestricted 24-hour rental. Split opening intervals, buffers and closures can make a request unavailable.

Advanced JSON retains opening exceptions, date rates and technical blocks while guided fields are saved. Only select advanced editing when you intend to replace the whole configuration object. Export a backup of its current JSON first. Do not invent property names; follow the included demo configuration and domain tests for the installed version.

Extras

Choose per reservation, per hour, per attendee or per item pricing. Choose children, adults or everyone for attendee pricing. Set a net minor-unit price, minimum and maximum quantities, whether required, allowed room IDs and allowed rental modes. Shared capacity 0 means unlimited; a positive capacity limits simultaneous inventory across bookings. Required or package-included extras are enforced on the server.

Packages

Select a room and rental mode. For a shift enter its exact key; for an hourly package enter duration in minutes. Set minimum included quantities for the published extras. Packages compose the configured room and extras; they do not create an independent arbitrary browser-supplied price or deposit. Publish all referenced items and verify a quote after editing.

4. Arrange the website

Use the Site Editor for navigation, logo, colors, templates and native WordPress blocks. Add the companion’s rooms, extras, packages, business information and booking blocks to your pages. The imported demo is an editable starting point. Keep booking rules in the plugin rather than theme templates. Deactivating the theme must not delete booking data.

Check phone and desktop layouts, keyboard navigation, visible focus, headings, labels and image alternative text after customization. Use WordPress’s RTL locale to inspect direction and alignment; translation support is not a claim that every third-party plugin or language has been tested.

5. Understand availability and reservation states

A visitor chooses room/date/mode, attendance and extras, reviews the current server quote, then provides the responsible adult’s contact information and accepts your terms. No child profile or card details are collected by the booking plugin. Changing a price or inventory between quote and reservation can require a fresh quote.

StateOperational meaning
holdTemporary capacity for payment checkout, until its expiry.
pendingManual request awaiting venue action, subject to its expiry.
confirmedAccepted reservation. Payment state remains separate.
expiredTemporary capacity has elapsed. Availability ignores expired holds even before scheduled cleanup runs.
cancelled / rejectedClosed booking. Cancellation does not fabricate a payment refund.
completedFinished service; completion is restricted until its end time.

Intervals are half-open: a rental ending at 12:00 can be adjacent to one starting at 12:00 when buffers permit. Invalid or ambiguous local times around daylight-saving changes are rejected. Inventory changes and payment confirmations use database transactions and resource locks.

In the reservation administration, review filters, dates, contact information, audit history and permitted state actions. A confirmed manual booking can still show pending payment. Paid rescheduling must retain the fiscal snapshot; a different amount or tax treatment requires an appropriate cancellation/refund and new reservation. Keep private reservation access links confidential.

6. WooCommerce full payment

Enable WooCommerce mode only after its currency, decimal precision, tax rates, business location, email and payment gateways are configured. Catalog amounts are always net minor units at the active currency precision. Switching precision or currency reinterprets configured integers; review every rate first. In manual mode the plugin’s configured tax basis points apply; in WooCommerce mode the manual tax setting does not override WooCommerce.

The venue service uses WooCommerce’s standard tax class and fixed business base location, including its configured postcode/city matching. This fiscal choice suits an on-site venue service; it does not implement tax advice or customer-location digital-service taxation. Confirm applicability with your adviser. WooCommerce supplies currency, precision, tax calculation and rounding settings. The quote stores its fiscal treatment. Changed rates, currency, coupons, fees or mismatching checkout totals cannot silently replace the accepted snapshot.

The plugin prepares one hidden virtual reservation carrier in the native WooCommerce cart. Checkout accepts one full reservation per order; it replaces all existing cart items when preparing that booking, so do not combine retail purchases with it. Checkout integrates with the official WooCommerce Store API and order APIs. Existing order retries use WooCommerce’s official payment link. No deposits, multi-booking baskets or custom card forms are implemented.

Use WooCommerce11.3.0 with HPOS and native Checkout Blocks. Payment gateways are configured separately in WooCommerce. Configure and test your chosen gateway in its sandbox before launch. Do not enter live card data into the development demo. A bank-transfer order does not prove money was received; venue/gateway confirmation must mark it paid. Paid, failed, cancelled and full-refund events synchronize through the domain service. Duplicate callbacks are idempotent and late failures cannot reverse paid state. A partial refund does not cancel the reservation or mark it fully refunded. A full refund updates payment state; review the reservation state separately.

If payment arrives after expiry or cancellation, the service records the receipt and reacquires availability safely. When capacity belongs to another booking, it does not displace it: inspect the audit/notification and arrange a refund or alternative. Never promise automatic provider refunds for this conflict. WooCommerce manages its own payment records and retention.

7. Backups, updates, privacy and removal

  1. Back up the database and wp-content together, including custom booking tables, WooCommerce data, uploads and child theme.
  2. Restore a copy on staging and verify existing private links, orders and future inventory before updating.
  3. Update parent theme and companion together from the reviewed release. Recheck translations, catalog prices, email, manual booking and sandbox checkout.
  4. Preserve child changes and do not overwrite the live database with the demo database. Roll back code and database together when necessary.

WordPress scheduled maintenance expires temporary records and anonymizes eligible closed reservations after the configured retention period. Configure reliable hosting cron for timely background jobs; availability does not depend on cron expiry. Outgoing mail needs working WordPress mail delivery; a local Mailpit capture is not proof of production deliverability.

WordPress Tools privacy export/erasure includes the booking plugin’s data. Active unresolved reservations can be retained; closed eligible records can be anonymized. WooCommerce records are handled separately. Review your privacy notice, retention obligations, email storage and backups before launch.

Deactivation retains business data. Uninstall is destructive only when the explicit purge setting is enabled; inspect uninstall.php and back up first. Deleting the theme is separate from deleting the plugin. Do not enable purge merely to switch themes.

8. Troubleshooting

  • No availability: check publication/active flag, timezone, attendance, weekly hours, closed dates, exceptions, notice, horizon, increment, buffers and shared extras. Check existing holds and their expiry.
  • Price changed: reload a quote after rate, currency, precision or tax edits. Never substitute a browser-supplied amount.
  • Checkout unavailable: check WooCommerce activation/mode, native checkout page, gateway availability, valid hold and matching tax snapshot. Exclude session, booking REST and cart/checkout responses from page caching.
  • Unexpected paid state: inspect WooCommerce order and booking audit together; a cancellation is not a refund and an expired booking can receive a late payment.
  • Email missing: inspect WordPress transport and outbox; verify sender/domain configuration with your host.
